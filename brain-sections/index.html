Oblique view of the unscaled Allen CCF. Markers are converted from the chosen coordinate system to the atlas. Each implant's tip sits at its ML/AP/depth target and runs up its tilt axis. Highlighted regions that are not on the surface show as silhouettes through the brain.

How coordinates are converted

The Allen CCF has no skull landmarks, so bregma is placed at CCF AP 5400 µm, DV 332 µm (IBL convention). ML 0 is the atlas midline. The CCF brain is slightly shorter (AP) and taller (DV) than a live stereotaxic brain; the Dorr scaling (AP ×1.087, DV ×0.952) corrects for this. Unscaled and scaled coordinates differ by up to ~0.5 mm in the cerebellum, so pick the one your coordinates come from. None of these is the Paxinos & Franklin atlas; expect mismatches of a few hundred µm.
