# Mouse brain sections

<https://sqbauer.github.io/brain-sections/>: a static page that draws a coronal or sagittal
section of the adult mouse brain at a given stereotaxic coordinate, or a dorsal (top-down) view,
optionally marks target sites (e.g. optic fibre or craniotomy positions by AP/ML on the dorsal
view), and exports the drawing as editable SVG (one named path per region) or PNG.

## Data

`data/` is generated by `tools/build_sections.py` from the Allen Mouse Brain Common Coordinate
Framework v3 (2017 annotation, 10 µm; [Wang et al. 2020, Cell](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cell.2020.04.007)).
Each region mask in each plane is Gaussian-blurred (σ = 10 µm), contoured at 0.5 and simplified
(Douglas–Peucker, 3.5 µm), so neighbouring regions share borders. Planes are every 50 µm: coronal
over the whole brain, sagittal over one hemisphere (the annotation is mirror-symmetric).
The dorsal view is the label of the topmost annotated voxel in every (AP, ML) column, i.e. the
brain seen from directly above along the CCF DV axis (no head pitch); at the full-atlas level,
cortical and cerebellar layers are merged into their parent area.
Three hierarchy levels are stored per plane: Allen "Major Divisions", Allen "Summary Structures"
(fiber tracts and ventricles grouped two/one levels below their roots) and the full annotation.

```bash
pip install numpy scipy scikit-image pynrrd
python tools/build_sections.py --cache /tmp/ccf --out data     # ~15 min, downloads ~33 MB
```

## Coordinates

- Bregma = CCF (AP 5400, DV 332) µm and ML 0 = the CCF mirror plane (5695 µm), as in
  [iblatlas](https://github.com/int-brain-lab/iblatlas) (`ALLEN_CCF_LANDMARKS_MLAPDV_UM`,
  `ALLEN_CCF_MIDLINE_ML_UM`).
- Optional axis scaling of the CCF to a stereotaxic brain, also from iblatlas: Dorr 2008 / "Needles"
  (AP ×1.087, DV ×0.952; default) or Qiu 2018 (ML ×0.952, AP ×1.031, DV ×0.885). The 5° pitch that
  Pinpoint adds to these is not applied (it would make sections oblique to the CCF planes).
- Marker depth is measured either from bregma or from the dorsal brain surface directly above the
  target; tracks are vertical.

These are approximations: the CCF is an average brain without skull landmarks, and none of these
coordinate systems is the Paxinos & Franklin atlas. Do not use the drawings for surgical planning
without checking against histology.
