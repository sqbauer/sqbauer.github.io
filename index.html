Staf Bauer

PhD candidate, Department of Neuroscience, Erasmus MC, Rotterdam

I study how the cerebellum controls movement, using chronic silicon-probe recordings and kinematic tracking in freely moving mice. Current work covers cerebellar encoding of locomotion and gait adjustment, sensorimotor adaptation, and cerebellar processing during sleep.
